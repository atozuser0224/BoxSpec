# Theme source and preview evidence

## Scope and handling

The packaged catalog contains 17 original BoxSpec token interpretations selected from public design-system references and public design showcase entries. It gives the Design Themes window varied, practical directions without importing remote HTML, scripts, CSS, fonts, or source-site assets into the trusted editor.

Every local preview in `packages/themes/catalog/previews/` is generated by BoxSpec from the small interpreted palette in `themes.json`. The preview `alt` and `credit` fields say that it is a generated token study and not a source screenshot. `source.previewUrl`, when present, is provenance metadata only; the runtime preview is local.

The palette, font-family fallback, radius, density, shadow, and motion descriptions are BoxSpec-authored interpretations. Names such as Material, Carbon, Fluent, and Primer identify the public reference. They do not claim that the reduced preset is the reference's exact design system or that the reference owner endorses BoxSpec.

## Reproducible collection

`scripts/themes/crawl-theme-sources.mjs` is a bounded metadata collector. Its input is the explicit allowlist in `packages/themes/catalog/sources.json`. It follows only the listed source and linked-original URL for each record, uses only HTTP(S), does not send credentials, and does not discover or traverse additional links. Limits are 24 records, 600,000 bytes per response, a 12-second timeout, and concurrency 3.

The capture at `2026-09-22T06:56:24.567Z` produced `packages/themes/catalog/source-snapshots.json`. Thirteen of 17 collection/reference pages returned HTTP 200. Four collection pages returned HTTP 403: two Land-book records and two Lapa Ninja records. The crawler did not retry with browser impersonation or otherwise bypass those controls. Three of their linked originals returned 200; Gusto also returned 403. The Gusto and blocked collection observations are retained only at the broad style/tag level established during public web research, and their blocked status remains explicit in the evidence.

Godly was evaluated as a possible collection source, but its public URL redirected to `recent.design` and the research browser rejected the redirect. It was omitted rather than bypassed. The Awwwards listing endpoint timed out during research; the two selected public detail pages were accessible and were fetched directly. No login, private page, paywall, or anti-bot challenge was bypassed.

`scripts/themes/build-theme-catalog.mjs` joins the fixed source records to curated interpretations, validates IDs and token namespaces, writes deterministically sorted `themes.json`, and generates one accessible SVG token study per preset. `scripts/themes/validate-theme-catalog.mjs` independently checks the wrapper, item count, IDs, HTTPS provenance, required presentation tokens, disclosure text, deterministic ordering, preview paths, and preview files.

## Rights and runtime boundary

Showcase screenshots and brand assets remain the property of their respective owners. They are linked for provenance and are not packaged. Official reference documentation remains subject to each publisher's terms. The GOV.UK reference separately publishes its own licensing information through the [GOV.UK Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/). BoxSpec ships only its JSON interpretation and newly generated SVG studies.

The catalog is suitable for visual direction selection, not for claiming a licensed copy of an external design system. Product teams adopting a direction should still review the referenced system's current brand, trademark, font, and component licenses before distributing any third-party asset or dependency.
